## Question

With the same F0 search and the same 8 real OpenFOAM calls, does the §26 promotion policy
reach 95% of the pooled-truth reference hypervolume on fewer CFD calls than random, greedy or
up-front allocation? And can the no-CFD arm reach it, in which case nothing can be saved?

## Action

`make adaptive SKIP_LLM=1`: 5 arms × 5 seeds, coarse mesh, 3 serial solvers side by side,
alongside M7 (4 workers) and M5. The exploratory `ai_adaptive` arm was not run — its live LLM
calls were not authorised and it is outside the H2 criterion. Nothing under `src/aether/` or
`configs/` was touched; source hash `c0a44bb0c14acd88` throughout. 3 h 44 min wall.

## Interpretation

This is a negative result for H2 in this design space, and the reason is physical before it
is statistical: after M3 the drag surface is already accurate where the objectives are
decided, so there is no model error near the front for CFD to remove, and an allocation
policy cannot save what nobody needs to spend. What limited every arm was the 200-evaluation
search, which stalled short of the high-bluntness corner where the front is — and an arm can
only promote what its search has found.

The study also had a design flaw that is mine to record and not to patch: the target was
fixed from a reference search five times longer than the arms' budget, and reachability was
never checked. The criterion stays as written. `adaptive` equalling `greedy` on half the calls
is true, and is not what was pre-declared.
