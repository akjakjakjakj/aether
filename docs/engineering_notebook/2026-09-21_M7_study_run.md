## Evidence

* Paired, same-draw: knee − peak-flux-only bondline = −14.31 K, s.d. 1.45 K, range
  −17.85 … −10.80 K, negative in 3000 / 3000 draws and in 24 / 24 epistemic branches; the two
  designs correlate at ρ = 0.992, so the independent-cloud s.d. of 10.0 K is the wrong ruler.
* Robust front: 46 designs, p95 flux 240.2–287.6 kW/m², p95 bondline 434.2–403.5 K; diameters
  3.308–3.323 m against 3.355–3.370 m nominal. Robust knee vs joint knee at nominal: flux
  +3.1%, bondline −0.46 K, P(any violation) 2.80% [1.68, 4.64] (n = 500) against 29.60%.
* Figure check (§34): `M7_attribution` mislabels two of three panels (`sharey=True` with a
  per-panel sort) and omits `joint_knee`; `M7_robust_vs_nominal` has no nominal front.

## Interpretation

**H0 stands under the declared uncertainty and H1 stands with one word removed.** The
peak-flux-only design is the hottest at the bondline in every shared draw, and the trade-off
reappears inside the chance-feasible robust front (30.6 K of p95 bondline across it). What
does not survive is calling the *nominal* optima feasible: an optimiser parks a design on its
constraint and any dispersion then fails it a third to half of the time. That the constraint
is a fence and the dispersion a judgment about a placeholder mass makes this a finding about
the constraint set, not about overheating — but it is still the reason §39's fourth row
exists, and it cost 3% of peak flux and essentially nothing at the bondline.

**The shopping list is two lists.** For peak flux, settle Ellison vs Zoby–Sullivan (T1,
resolvable, 71% of the variance at the knee). For the bondline — the objective the project is
about — the dominant terms are an insulator conductivity that is a placeholder and a heating
multiplier above 86 km that is a judgment. Better nose-radius evidence would barely move it.

**The optimiser gamed the inner sample, mildly, and the pre-declared check measured it.**
All 46 designs at exactly 1/32 is the signature. The check was designed before the run for
exactly this, passed on its declared terms, and still surfaced one marginal design. Tightening
the criterion now, after seeing that, would be tuning; it is recorded instead.

Everything here is a lower bound on the real uncertainty: G2′ has no distribution.
