## 0. What this run is, and is not

This is an uncertainty study of a **model**, not of a vehicle. It propagates the spread that `configs/uncertainty.yaml` declares, through the assumptions `ASSUMPTIONS.md` declares. It does not and cannot cover a model-form error that is not on that list — most importantly gate **G2′** (Sutton–Graves' catalycity, hot-wall and radiation assumptions, where catalycity alone is worth about a factor of two) is `LIMITED` and untouched here. An output distribution from this study is therefore a **lower bound on the real uncertainty**, and the §7 limitations section lists what is missing rather than leaving it implied.

The mechanism is shape invariance: every design produces very nearly the same normalised depth profile, so any weighted depth integral of it recovers the scale factor and nothing more. ## 7. Limitations

**7 of 9 active inputs are engineering judgment** (`atmospheric_density`, `vehicle_mass`, `entry_flight_path_angle`, `high_altitude_heating_model`, `tps_conductivity`, `tps_specific_heat`, `cd_fidelity0_judgment`). Their spreads are declared, not sourced, and the width of every output distribution here is conditional on them. The two inputs that are T1 — the effective-nose-radius model form and the Sutton–Graves constant — are the ones this project measured itself, and they are not the small ones.

**Not propagated in this run:** `cd_surrogate_gp`, `cd_discretisation`, `cd_perfect_gas_model_form`, `cd_base_drag`. At Fidelity 0 the four sourced C_D terms (GP predictive σ with its stored 1.65 inflation, the M2 discretisation band, the base-drag band and the declared perfect-gas half-band) do not exist, and they are replaced by a single unsourced ±10% band on a constant C_D. That substitution is not equivalent and the C_D-related numbers here should not be carried into a Fidelity-1 discussion.

**Not covered at all.** Gate G2′ (aeroheating model form: catalycity, hot wall, radiation) is `LIMITED` and is not in the inventory; catalycity alone is reported at roughly a factor of two. A-TPS-2's temperature-independent conductivity, A-TPS-3's adiabatic back face, A-HEAT-4's stagnation-point-only treatment and A-TRAJ-1's non-rotating Earth are structural model-form choices with no distribution attached. The deceleration limit itself is an **open student decision** (A-LIM-1b): 12 g corresponds to no documented sustained-g curve, and adopting the NASA-STD-3001 deconditioned curve would empty the feasible region. Every chance constraint on `max_g` below is therefore a statement about a placeholder limit.

**The atmosphere above 86 km.** Gate G1A′ is `PASS` for five transcribed rows and `LIMITED` for everything else, the interpolation between rows is not the species-diffusion model USSA-76 actually defines there, and NR-14 measured that 5–20% of a feasible design's heat load comes from that band. Two inputs here — the >86 km density dispersion rows and the high-altitude heating multiplier — are engineering judgment about exactly that region, so the study quantifies the consequence of a known weakness without removing it.
